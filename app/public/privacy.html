Dishaara privacy

Test version for the AI Synergy Hackathon 2026 by team Argus. Last changed 22 Oct 2026.

Without an account

You can plan a day without signing in. Your choices stay in your own browser.

When you sign in with Google

We keep your Google account id, your first name and your email address, so we know who wrote a post, comment or feedback. We do not get your Google password, contacts or anything else.

What we store for your account

Your interests and travel preferences, your saved plans, the stamps you collect and your points, and the posts, comments, likes, saves, follows and reports you make. We never store your religion: lines in your preferences that state one are dropped.

Location

Location is off until you turn it on. It is used only to pick your city, to check a stamp at a place, and for the emergency button. For a stamp we keep the result and the time, not your route. If you press the emergency button, your location at that moment is shared with the Dishaara moderators.

Voice and chat

Chat questions go to our server and to Groq to write the answer. Read-aloud text goes to ElevenLabs. Voice input goes to Groq to turn speech into text. We do not keep your voice recordings.

Deleting your data

Email adictiveofficial@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will delete your account and everything linked to it.
