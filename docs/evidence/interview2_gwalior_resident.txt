# Interview date: 14 Oct 2026. Answers: written (text), exactly as below. Consent: taken (confirmed by Aditya, 15 Oct 2026).

**1. Concentration (PS-2: preference, awareness, time or transport?)**

- When relatives or friends visit, where do they want to go, and where do you take them?
- Which places do you think are worth seeing that outsiders almost never visit? Why don't they?
- Do visitors ask about places beyond the fort and Jai Vilas, or do they not know those exist?
- If a visitor has 6 hours, what would you plan for them? Would you add anything that tourists usually skip?

**2. Information (PS-1 root)**
- When you send someone somewhere, how do *you* check whether it's open and what it costs?
- Can you recall times when you, or people you know, went somewhere and it was closed, the price was different, or the ticket counter refused cash or UPI? When was that?
- Have you ever heard tourists confused about separate tickets inside the fort (ASI vs state palaces)?
- Have you seen Google or online information about Gwalior that you know is wrong? Give examples.

**3. Getting around (PS-3)**
- What do autos and e-rickshaws really charge from the station or Bada to the fort, Jai Vilas, Tansen tomb and Sun Temple?
- Is overcharging tourists common? How does it happen?
- How do people get *up* to the fort? Is there an easy way at Urwai Gate or the main gate?
- Is there any city bus that tourists can actually use to reach these sites?
- How long does each leg realistically take at different times of day, including traffic hotspots?

**4. Residents and sensitive sites (PS-4; resident burden is your biggest evidence gap)**
- When the fort or other busy spots are crowded, what changes for people living nearby: parking, traffic, noise, litter?
- Have you seen visitors write on walls, spit, litter or behave disrespectfully? Where, and how often?
- Are there religious places or neighbourhoods where locals would *not* want more tourists? Why?
- Which lesser-known sites have no staff, no guard or no signboard?
- What happens at the Tansen and Ghaus tombs during events and on normal days?
- Would locals welcome more visitors at quieter sites, or resist? What would they want first?

**5. Small operators, food and crafts**
- Where do locals eat that tourists never find?
- Do the famous street vendors stay in one spot, or keep moving?
- Do they use UPI? Are they on Google Maps?
- Are there craft workers (stone carving, durries, etc.) a visitor could actually visit? Where?
- How do those small businesses get customers today?
- Do you know any guides personally? What do they charge, and how do tourists find them?

**6. Time and stay (PS-5)**
- Do most visitors you've seen come for the day, or stay overnight? Do any come from Agra?
- What is there to do in the evening outside December (light-and-sound show, markets, anything else)?
- Is music, the UNESCO City of Music title, visible anywhere in daily life outside Tansen Samaroh?

**7. Change over time**
- What has changed in the last 5–10 years: new attractions, closures, crowds, roads, the ropeway, e-buses?
- What would you tell the government is the single biggest tourism problem in Gwalior?
